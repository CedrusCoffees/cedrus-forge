# Cedrus Forge

> AI-powered platform for acquiring local businesses through personalized digital audits.

## The Problem

Millions of local businesses have poor digital presence:

- No website
- Poor Google Business profile
- No online ordering
- No WhatsApp automation
- No digital strategy

Most don't know what they're missing.

## The Solution

Cedrus Forge automatically:

1. Import businesses
2. Analyzes their digital presence
3. Generates a personalized AI audit
4. Creates a demo website
5. Launches email & WhatsApp outreach
6. Converts prospects into customers

## Features

- 📍 Google Maps Business Import
- 🤖 AI Digital Score
- 📄 Personalized PDF Audits
- 🌐 Instant Demo Website Generator
- 📧 Automated Email Outreach
- 💬 WhatsApp Campaigns
- 📈 Lead Pipeline Dashboard

## Tech Stack

- Next.js

- PostgreSQL
- OpenAI
- Docker
